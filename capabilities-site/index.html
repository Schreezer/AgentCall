05 / Personal setup

What installing Caller changes

Install the signed iPhone build, allow notifications, and pair the app with Hermes. That gives your agent a private channel to reach this installation.

1

Install and register

Open Caller on a physical iPhone. Let it register for standard notifications and VoIP pushes; confirm its status says Ready. A signed build and working APNs relay are required.

2

Pair with Hermes

Use the app’s short-lived setup code. Hermes receives a revocable token scoped to this installation; the code and token should stay out of public links and screenshots.

3

Choose what should ring

Ask for a Caller app call when you want a VoIP/CallKit conversation. Ask for your phone number when you want a standard cellular ring, after Vobiz activation. The app is optional for that direct PSTN route.

4

Read inbound results

After the DID path is enabled and an inbound call ends, a paired Caller app can receive a short, digit-redacted excerpt marked as unverified caller speech, or a generic fallback. Ask Hermes for the fuller bounded report or digest.

06 / Important boundaries

What the phone can—and cannot—do

Can I send only unknown Jio callers to the agent?

Not with Jio’s documented forwarding controls. Busy forwarding is the intended route; No Answer may be a fallback. Either condition applies to every caller who meets it. Jio does not provide an “unknown callers only” forwarding condition.

Can I decline one call and send it to the agent?

That is unverified on this Jio SIM. Apple says declining may send a call to voicemail or disconnect it, so it cannot be assumed to trigger Busy forwarding. Caller has no per-call button that transfers a cellular call or its audio to the agent. Test actual Busy and decline behavior on the SIM after the direct Vobiz number works.

Will the caller hear a natural ringing tone?

Before answer, ringback is controlled by the phone network. After the authenticated Vobiz audio stream opens, the bridge now plays a more recognizable in-call connecting cadence through GPT Live startup and clears it before speech. This is post-answer audio, not carrier ringback. Its real loudness and timing still need a live call test.

Can GPT Live start before the phone is answered?

Not through the current Vobiz integration. Its documented StartApp callback occurs at answer; the bridge starts that call’s GPT Live session after the authenticated media stream begins. Prewarming before dialing has been requested to reduce delay but is not implemented.

Will it speak any language the caller uses?

The inbound prompt now asks GPT Live to follow the caller’s understood language or ask for clarification. The first fixed greeting remains in English. Real multilingual carrier audio has not been tested, so there is no verified guarantee for every language or dialect.

Can a stranger make Hermes do work through a call?

No. The incoming assistant is restricted to taking a message. It has no private Hermes context or general tools. The owner can later ask Hermes to read a bounded, redacted call report.
