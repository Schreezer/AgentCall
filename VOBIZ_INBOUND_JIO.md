## Intended path

1. A caller dials the owned Vobiz number directly, or Jio conditionally forwards a busy cellular call to it after the direct DID path is proven.
2. A dedicated Vobiz voice Application posts its **answer** and **hangup** callbacks to the isolated `vobiz-outbound` Worker. The Worker authenticates the callback, checks the owned destination and bridge readiness, records the call, and returns VobizXML for a bidirectional audio stream.
3. The separate DebianBat Vobiz bridge joins that stream to Codex App Server using Hermes's `openai-codex` credential pool and the owner's ChatGPT/Codex authentication tokens. GPT Live handles speech; its substantive text handoffs use the pinned `gpt-6-sol` thread. Sol is the text reasoning model, not the voice model. A short greeting or acknowledgement may be generated by GPT Live without a Sol handoff. The first two-way carrier conversation is verified, but a substantive Sol handoff is still unproven. There is no OpenAI API key or alternate voice provider in this path. See [bridge design](integrations/hermes/urgent-caller/VOBIZ_CODEX_BRIDGE.md).
4. The agent identifies itself as an AI assistant, asks why the person called, and takes a bounded message. It does not expose private Hermes context or use Hermes tools for an unknown caller. After the call, the deployed bridge first fsyncs a bounded, redacted quote, then gives only redacted caller turns to a fresh restricted `gpt-6-sol` thread for up to 20 seconds. A grounded result is labeled `Likely reason (inferred, unverified):`; timeout, error, cancellation, or restart keeps the attributed quote, and no useful speech produces the generic fallback. The resulting owner alert goes to the fixed Hermes Telegram chat, with the paired Caller relay as fallback when Hermes delivery is unset. Synthetic Sol inference is proven; verify the phone-derived inference and delivery separately, because neither an ended audio stream nor command acceptance proves that the result was useful or read.

Vobiz documents voice [Applications](https://www.vobiz.ai/docs/applications/create-application), [number attachment](https://www.vobiz.ai/docs/applications/attach-number), and [bidirectional streams](https://www.vobiz.ai/docs/concepts/streaming-websockets). Attaching the number immediately sends **all calls to that DID** to the selected Application. `Hermes_Inbound` now has the DID attached and uses explicit, distinct `answer_url` and `hangup_url` values; Vobiz otherwise defaults `hangup_url` to `answer_url`. Keep `default_number_app` and `default_endpoint_app` false, and retain the previous binding state for rollback.

## Limits of the iPhone and Jio path

[Jio offers](https://www.jio.com/help/faq/mobile/services/hd-voice/what-are-different-types-of-call-forwarding/) unconditional, no-answer, busy, and not-reachable forwarding. It does **not** list “unknown caller only” forwarding. The initial Jio policy for this personal route is **Busy only**, covering every caller who meets that carrier condition. Confirm actual behavior on this SIM because [call waiting](https://www.jio.com/help/faq/mobile/services/hd-voice/what-call-management-services-are-offered-by-jio/) may present a second call while the line is in use. [Apple describes Decline](https://support.apple.com/en-in/guide/iphone/iph3c9947bf/ios) as sending a call to voicemail or disconnecting it in some regions; neither Apple nor Jio documents Decline as a guaranteed Busy-forwarding trigger. Do not claim that tapping Decline hands one call to GPT Live until a controlled Jio test proves it.

The Caller iPhone app's PushKit/CallKit UI handles **its own VoIP calls**; it does not capture or answer a Jio cellular call. Apple's [CallKit](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/callkit?changes=_2) and [Call Directory](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/callkit/identifying-and-blocking-calls?changes=l_10_8_7&language=objc) interfaces cover app VoIP calls and caller identification/blocking. For this feature, the server-side agent answers the Vobiz leg and Hermes Telegram is the planned owner-message path; Caller remains the fallback if Hermes delivery is unset. Carrier forwarding covers Jio voice calls, not personal WhatsApp calls.

## Enable in two stages

### Stage 1: Direct DID calls only

- Confirm that the purchased DID is owned, accepts ordinary incoming voice calls, and is an eligible **10-digit Indian mobile or landline** destination for Jio. [Jio's activation FAQ](https://www.jio.com/help/faq/mobile/services/hd-voice/how-can-i-activate-call-forwarding-service/) excludes international, BSNL, and the subscriber's own Jio number. Check number type and any forwarding charges or restrictions with the providers before using the personal line.
- Migrations `0002_inbound.sql`, `0003_caller_notification_outbox.sql`, `0004_notification_message.sql`, and `0005_notification_quarantine.sql` are applied, and inbound is enabled only for this controlled direct-DID pilot. The fixed callback routes remain HMAC-only. The active Application uses Answer and Hangup URLs suffixed by one confidential 43-character, 256-bit bearer, and the deployed token routes also require provider HMAC. Keep the bearer only as a Cloudflare secret and in Vobiz; never put its value in source, logs, screenshots, or this file. Vobiz documents `Event`, `auth_id`, and `CallUUID` for application callbacks; `Direction` and `To` may be omitted, but if either is supplied it must be unique and correct. The routes reject invalid signatures, replay changes, wrong accounts, wrong or duplicate identity fields, and malformed or duplicate call IDs. Confirm the exact route forms against the deployed [`vobiz-outbound` README](vobiz-outbound/README.md).
- DebianBat's standalone bridge service and public route are installed, inbound-enabled, and publicly healthy. The Worker checks that readiness before answering and fails closed when authentication, identity, capacity, or bridge checks fail. Follow [bridge deployment notes](integrations/hermes/urgent-caller/VOBIZ_CODEX_BRIDGE.md) and the Hermes host runbook. Do not start a second Hermes Telegram poller or restart its gateway for this standalone bridge.
- The Vobiz `Hermes_Inbound` Application already has explicit POST Answer and Hangup token callbacks and owns the DID attachment. The 1 October attempts reached the Worker but were rejected before media; place one controlled post-patch direct call and inspect callback acceptance, the WSS stream, call record, and owner alert without logging bearer URLs or signature values. If callbacks are rejected or absent, speech fails, or the resulting record is inconsistent, immediately set the Worker inbound flag to false and redeploy before investigating.
- Call the DID **directly** from another phone or network. This first real call must confirm the Answer and Hangup callbacks, AI disclosure, intelligible two-way speech, clean hangup, correct call record, phone-derived Sol reason, and direct Hermes Telegram message. For a clearly stated purpose, require the exact `Likely reason (inferred, unverified):` label and verify that the result is supported by caller speech. If either callback or media fails, immediately disable the Worker inbound flag and redeploy. Separately exercise the 20-second timeout or model failure and confirm the already-durable `Caller said (unverified):` quote is delivered instead. Test a caller who gives no useful reason and one who speaks in the expected non-English language; check redaction and generic fallback. Also test no answer or bridge unavailable, occupied bridge, invalid webhook signatures, and duplicate callbacks. The call record and notification must distinguish command acceptance from evidence that the owner actually read the message. Retest after relevant Codex or bridge upgrades.

### Stage 2: Jio Busy, only after Stage 1 passes

- With the owner at the Jio SIM and able to test and roll back, use [MyJio's documented flow](https://www.jio.com/help/faq/mobile/services/hd-voice/how-can-i-activate-call-forwarding-service/): Profile → Mobile Settings → Service Settings → Call Forwarding → Voice → choose **Busy**, complete SIM verification, and enter the Vobiz DID as a 10-digit number. Jio says the iPhone settings surface supports unconditional forwarding only, so do not use it for this conditional policy.
- Place controlled calls to the personal Jio line from a separate number. Verify a normally answered call still reaches the owner. Separately test a call while the Jio line is already in use and a call that the owner explicitly declines; record the outcomes with call waiting on and off if the first run is ambiguous. A declined call may go to voicemail or disconnect instead of forwarding. Check the agent's greeting, recorded reason, Hermes Telegram message, and actual Jio/Vobiz call records. Do not treat a Worker `200`, Hermes command success, or a Vobiz `completed` status alone as end-to-end proof.
- If Decline does not forward, discuss **No Answer** as a separate later policy: letting a call ring out would apply to every unanswered caller. Keep a note of the active condition and previous DID binding so rollback is immediate. Do not enable unconditional forwarding merely to make a test pass.

## Rollback

1. Jio Busy forwarding is currently off. If it is enabled in a later stage, disable it first through [MyJio](https://www.jio.com/help/faq/mobile/services/hd-voice/how-can-i-deactivate-call-forwarding-service/) after SIM verification; Jio also documents per-condition deactivation short codes on that page. Test from another number that calls no longer divert.
2. If direct DID routing is failing, restore the recorded previous Vobiz Application binding. If there was no prior binding, [detach the DID](https://www.vobiz.ai/docs/applications/detach-number); Vobiz says detachment leaves the number owned but stops routing it to that Application. Test direct DID behavior after the change.
3. Disable new inbound admission in the isolated Worker. Let active calls finish or end them deliberately before stopping the standalone bridge. Preserve call records for diagnosis; do not alter Hermes's gateway lifecycle for this rollback.

The direct DID and Jio stages are separate: no carrier setting is needed to prove that Vobiz can receive a call and the Codex agent can speak to its caller.
